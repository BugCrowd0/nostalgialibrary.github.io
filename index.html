A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Made by a curious kid from a small town.

Hey, I'm Jayden I'm 13 years old, and I'm from a small town. I've been interested in computers for a long time. I used to imagine a day when you could just type something into a computer and it would create it for you. It always seemed like something from the future.

That idea is a big part of why I wanted to make this project. I used AI as a tool to help me plan and build parts of Nostalgia Library, and I used the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine to help bring old websites and pieces of internet history back to life.

I made this because I think it's interesting to see what the internet used to look like, even if you weren't around when some of these websites were popular. I hope you find something cool, strange, or nostalgic while exploring.

— JAYDEN M. · CREATOR OF NOSTALGIA LIBRARY

WELCOME TO THE ARCHIVE

Hi. Welcome to the Nostalgia Library.

This is a little time machine for the old internet. The library uses a curated list of websites and the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine to find pages from years ago.

Some places in here have disappeared. Others are older versions of websites you might still use today. Every time you discover a page, the site asks the archive for a snapshot from around that site's era.

A little heads-up: archived pages can be broken, missing images, or blocked from showing inside this window. That is normal for old web archives. You can open a page in a new tab if needed.
